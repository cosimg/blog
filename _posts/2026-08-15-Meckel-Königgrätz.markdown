**The Battle of Königgrätz as a Meckel Kriegsspiel**

For the 160th anniversary of the Battle of Königgrätz Austrian television produced a documentary on the battle. It included a small section briefly explaining the Prussian Kriegsspiel. It also featured a setup of the whole battlefield using the apparat of the Meckel Kriegsspiel on a copy of an original 1867 Kriegsspiel map.

The documentary (in German) can be found here:

[https://www.arte.tv/de/videos/123937-000-A/habsburg-gegen-hohenzollern/][https://www.arte.tv/de/videos/123937-000-A/habsburg-gegen-hohenzollern/)

The section on the Kriegsspiel starts at 41:00.
